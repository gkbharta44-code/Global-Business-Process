A Global Humanitarian and Bio-Mimicry Initiative

1. Overview & Seed Capital

USD 2.07 Billion raised as seed capital over 2 years via monetizing SBLCs funds a globally decentralized cloud, with a main hub in Singapore and a managed back-office plus disaster-recovery collocation server in India, connected concurrently to the decentralized cloud across global locations to achieve zero data loss.

  • Introduce ERC-20 "Delper Tokens" as the medium of exchange for all platform revenue.
  • Goal: onboard 2 billion families (community commerce) within 5 years.
  • 3 KYC'd members per family (ages 18–29 and 60–75) view ads and complete surveys — up to USD 823 per family per 30 days for 90 hours of activity.
  • Participants also maintain Delper Ecosystem services: education, mentoring, business incubation, vocational training, smart-city traffic, EV charging roads for seamless vehicle charging on the move, upkeep, rooftop and floating solar, rooftop micro wind power generators, hydrogen/plasma power generation, food security, water management, peacekeeping, and recreation.

3. Recursive Buyer/Seller Supply Chain (Layers)

Six recursive layers are proposed for revenue generation: Originators, Processors, Stockists, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Retailers.

  • Buy tax: 0.5% — held in a liquid pool supporting Delper Token intrinsic value.
  • Sell tax: 0.5% — same liquidity purpose.
  • Up to 18% transaction charge per layer for sales conducted on the platform.

Projected: USD 1.067 Trillion per annum in supply-chain commission by Year 5.

4. Franchise Compression (Year 6 Onward) — DOFO Model

From Year 6, the plan is to take direct control of the supply chain (capturing the price difference between Originator and Retailer) and compress the recursive layers into 3 billion Delper-Owned, Franchise-Operated (DOFO) local franchises globally within 5 years.

  • Investment per franchise: USD 250,000 ($150,000 fixed capital + $100,000 working capital).
  • 66% of franchise income from sale of onboarded grocery/daily-use products.
  • 33% from other services: indoor games, beauty parlour, quick-service restaurant.
  • Platform pays franchises up to 10% management fee per transaction; 3% of that services MTN interest over 20 years.

Projected: USD 8.892 Trillion per annum supply-chain profit from Year 6.

6. Smart Cities & MTN Financing

From Year 6, the plan proposes issuing 3% p.a., 20-year MTNs at USD 150,000 each, based on local franchise operations at 2.22x DSCR, to raise and service USD 450 Trillion for building 1,470 of 4,400 planned smart cities (Years 5–11): 2,200 new cities (5,000 acres each) and 2,200 refurbished/retrofit cities, each costing USD 306 Billion and targeted to earn 15% gross revenue p.a. By Year 21 the remaining 2,930 cities are to be completed, housing all 8 billion platform users.

  • Debt raised by Year 8: USD 450 Trillion.
  • Projected smart-city rental income by Year 21: USD 2.42 Quadrillion per annum.

Physical Infrastructure Build-Out

4,400 smart cities × 22 structural towers = 96,800 towers. At 1,850,000 m³ geopolymer per tower (density 2.4 t/m³), total mass is ~429.8 billion tons, built over an 18-year (6,574.5-day) horizon — implying ~65.37 million tons/day globally, or ~14,857 tons/day per city (roughly 3.6× current global cement output). Estimated annual infrastructure investment: USD 58.6–70.9 Trillion (~51–61% of current global GDP). USD 540 Trillion (50% of the low-end CAPEX boundary) is proposed to come from reallocating legacy global expense ledgers over the 18-year build.

These cities are built by manufacturing the required construction materials onsite. They are earthquake resistant and built with an anthill replica design for natural cooling.

6. Enterprise Blockchain — Hyperledger Fabric

A permissioned consortium blockchain (not public) is recommended given 235 sovereign jurisdictions' transaction-cost, privacy (GDPR, data residency), and throughput constraints.

  • Private channels isolate bonded-release data between specific countries and their customs authorities.
  • Raft-based consensus (not Proof-of-Work) delivers thousands of TPS per channel with immediate finality.
  • Chaincode (smart contracts) automatically triggers customs duty release on IoT delivery confirmation.

8. Institutional Pitch — Franchise Infrastructure Deposit

Framed for Managing Directors, CIOs, and sovereign asset allocators: a proposed 705-hub network across 235 countries connecting 700 million SKUs from 3 billion product franchises to 2 billion consumers. Structure pitched to master franchise partners:

  • USD 10 million infrastructure deposit per hub per country, 20-year contract.
  • Years 1–3: 100% legal title to physical/logistics assets retained by the partner as collateral; 18% p.a. fixed yield.
  • Year 4: automated liquidity exit via a proposed USD 2.35 Billion corporate MTN syndicate (named banks: Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, HSBC); full principal returned, asset titles transfer to Delper.
  • Years 5–9: 5% p.a. passive "goodwill yield" continues on zero remaining locked capital.
  • Cited resilience: 2.31x DSCR under a stated 15% macroeconomic stress scenario; 30% operational cost cap.

Executive Summary

The logistics blueprint frames a shift from reactive supply-chain operations to a predictive, self-optimizing utility built on AI, IoT, and enterprise blockchain (Hyperledger Fabric), anchored by an Active-Active Singapore control tower and a read-only India disaster-recovery center. On the stated volume and cost assumptions, the model reports a $36.75B capital base generating $488.50B in annual net operating income, breaking even at roughly 1.76% of designed volume.
